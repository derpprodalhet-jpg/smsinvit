## Codex Switcher

The invitee CLI execution design is adapted from
[VallierDev/codex-switcher](https://github.com/VallierDev/codex-switcher),
commit `5a863d8ab273fe85fa4b1a48fd2b27175e00c3c0`, particularly
`src-tauri/src/lib.rs::open_codex_terminal`. SmsInvit retains its own automatic
registration, CLI completion checks, and credential rotation persistence. The
invitation completion check is a nonempty CLI assistant reply; it does not
establish that an upstream reward has been received.
